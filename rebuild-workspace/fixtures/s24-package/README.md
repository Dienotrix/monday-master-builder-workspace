# s24-package (calibration fixture, A15)

A handed-over package: five recordings from another seller, taken in as one set and graded once. The client is a print and sign shop. The handing-over seller ran a discovery call, two demos, an internal call review with no client present, and a pricing call; her sales engineer drove the screen on the demos and sat in the review. The user attended none of them. Everything in it is invented. Guards reversals R6 (a cross-speaker conflict is never resolved by recency) and R7 (a structural disagreement is a fork, not a conflict).

## The export grammar

A call-recording platform export none of s1 to s23 carries: a metadata block between two rules (Recording, Recording ID, Date, Duration, Host, Participants, Transcript, Highlights), topic dividers `── Title ──`, and turns as a label line `Speaker Name · mm:ss` followed by exactly one text line, with no end times. Speaker labels come from the meeting accounts, so the roster's display names differ from the labels in two files (Gwendolyn / Gwen, Guadalupe / Lupe). The Highlights bullets are machine-written summaries that carry mis-merged and seller-side figures and are never evidence. Every location in the key is `rec-NNNN, mm:ss Speaker Name (role)`, from the label line the quoted text line sits under; the two seller-side speakers carry `seller` and `sales engineer` as their roles.

## Conventions and deviations worth knowing

- The top-level `call_type` is `discovery`, the type the set is graded under (`discovery-check` chains once over the package); the per-file types, discovery, demo, call-review, demo and pricing, are in `intake.files`, using the thirteen labels of ARCHITECTURE.md 7.1 and, for the type header, the tokens `shared/record-map.md` pins (the 8.3 step 5 values in their hyphenated form), with each file's reasoning in a `_note` beside the field.
- `attendees[].side` uses `seller` for the handing-over seller and her sales engineer and `bot` for the recording assistant, beside `client`. No one is `consultant`: the user is on no call, and the key says "the user" throughout.
- The key uses `met-with-residual` (R7, R10) and the per-criterion `asked` flag with `asked_quote`. It does not use `not-obtainable` or `advisory`: the P4 cost figure has no other room to route to, and every call type resolves. The seat dimension on the seller-side calls is proposed as partner-led with vendor-led named as the other candidate, since the seller's firm is not stated; on the two demos the same-side screen-direction signal fires (the seller directing her sales engineer's screen by name), so co-sell is named as a third candidate and then set aside on 'our demo workspace' and 'On our demo account, yes.', which put the two on one firm's side. Accepted behaviours allow any of the three as long as the signal's quote is recorded, the candidates are named and the user is asked.
- Unlike s1 to s10, the transcripts state their own dates, in the metadata block; that is the point of the fixture. All five fit July and August 2026 and the next step, 27 August at 9:00, collides with no other fixture's meeting. No time zone is stated anywhere.
- Rows, pains, objections, traps and every intake entry print on one line each; the intake block's files and claims are long lines by design. The 25 fields keep the s1 to s10 set and order; `intake` and `readiness` follow them. In each file's type header the direction dimension is named `direction` (renamed from `direction_of_show` in the fix pass).
- Tool references are category words throughout, at most one category per sentence, in the transcripts and in the key alike: the estimating system, email, a shared drive, a spreadsheet, the whiteboard, a group text, the accounting package, a phone app, a task-board tool. The platform's tier name (Pro) appears in the pricing call and the review because a tier name is a commercial marker the type header is required to detect; it is the seller's proposal, never the client's tier.
- Real place names (two towns and a state) appear as they do in s1 to s10; the client's street is never named, on purpose, because a street plus a trade would point at a real business. Person, organisation and customer names were checked against s1 to s23 and s25, and against the web, and are unique to this fixture.
- The transcripts carry the ordinary mess of an automatic export without announcing it: fillers, stutters and false starts, two contractions written as 'gonna' and 'gotta', one turn cut off with a trailing ' --' (rec-4409 05:29) and three misheard names. No evidence quote spans an added filler, a stutter or a misheard name (the key's `transcript_format` quotes two stutters as examples, which is description, not evidence); where a quoted turn carries one, it sits outside the marks. The fix pass added four more on the seller's side (rec-4402 00:31 and 05:27, rec-4397 01:14, rec-4409 02:46), inside existing turns, outside every quoted span, and with no turn added or renumbered; the word counts in the key's `transcript_format` follow them.
