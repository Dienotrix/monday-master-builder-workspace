## Conventions and deviations worth knowing

- The key uses two of the calibration statuses, `met-with-residual` (R5) and `not-obtainable` (P4 Cost inputs), and the per-criterion `asked` flag with `asked_quote`. It does not exercise `advisory`, because the call type resolves cleanly.
- Every list entry is printed on one line to stay under the 400-line budget; inside the `intake` block, objects two levels down are printed compact as well. The 25 fields keep the s1 to s10 order; `intake` and `readiness` follow them.
- The three swallowed assents are ground truth for the fixture's talk-share figures (six client words labelled Consultant) and are asserted in the key only as non-evidence with a re-ask; the key never says who spoke them, because a parser cannot know.
- The call type header carries the tokens `shared/record-map.md` pins for the type header (the ARCHITECTURE.md 8.3 step 5 values in their hyphenated form, with the fixture's reasoning in a `_note` beside each field) and the labels of 7.1 verbatim.
- Tool references are category words throughout, one software category per sentence, in the transcript and the key alike: the inventory system, the accounting system, the shared drive, a spreadsheet, email, a shared calendar, a phone. The platform is named once, lowercase, in the Consultant's question at L203 and the client's answer at L204, about having no account.
- The Consultant appears only as `Consultant`, with no personal name and no gendered pronoun. Our colleague carries a fictional name because the transcript needs a second labelled speaker on our side; the user is never one of the people in the file.
- The consortium, the museum in the claim, the handler with the replaced phone, the insurer, the broker and the vendor are all unnamed and stay unnamed; the managing director, the finance manager and the crew chief are first names only.
- No town, region or country is named; the second store carries an invented place-style name. Person, organisation and place names were checked against every other fixture folder and the design documents and collide with none.
- The titles "operations director", "managing director" and "finance manager" are shorthand used by this README and the key; none is spoken. The transcript says only that Constance hosts the call and approves up to twenty-five thousand, that Lionel is the founder and that Mireille is "in finance". The key's attendee roles and alias map say so.
