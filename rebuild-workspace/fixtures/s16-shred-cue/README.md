# s16-shred-cue (calibration fixture, C58c)

A first-hand discovery call exported as WebVTT with individually timed units, the cue-export shred case of REAL-CALL-LESSONS.md section 6 row C58 (c) and BUILD-PLAN.md section 4.2 row s16. The export marks nothing and looks clean, yet it wedges one-word units of one speaker inside another speaker's run at zero-millisecond gaps, re-emits the last words of a unit as the first words of the next, truncates a figure mid-word, labels one speaker with a bare initial, and carries one name-shaped unit that matches nobody. Roughly two fifths of adjacent units share a label. Fictional client: a sign fabrication and installation shop of about forty-eight people, run by the founder's daughter with the founder still in every morning. Everything in it is invented.

No location in the key crosses a label change.

## Conventions and deviations worth knowing

- The key uses two of the calibration statuses, `met-with-residual` and `not-obtainable`, and the per-criterion `asked` flag with `asked_quote`. It does not exercise `advisory`, because the call type resolves cleanly; no calibration fixture carries a positive `advisory` row, because every fixture's type resolves; the value's positive case is the constructed unresolvable fragment of BUILD-PLAN.md section 4.3, item 8 (H50).
- Every list entry (a rubric row, a pain, an objection, an intake entry, a readiness entry) is printed on one line, the stage-1 convention that kept the key under 400 lines; the fix pass's `readiness` block takes the file to 537 lines with the same convention. The field set and order of the 25 fields are unchanged from s1 to s10; `intake` and `readiness` follow them. The key is UTF-8 since the fix pass (the en dash in the workspace names); the transcript stays ASCII.
- The transcript file is `transcript.txt` with WebVTT inside, as s4 and s8 are; the label sits in the cue payload as `Label: text`, the s4 dialect, not the `<v>` voice tag of s8.
- Call type and the type header use the tokens `shared/record-map.md` pins for the type header (the ARCHITECTURE.md 8.3 step 5 values in their hyphenated form, with the fixture's reasoning in a `_note` beside each field) and the labels of 7.1 verbatim.
- Tool references are category words throughout, at most one category per sentence, in the transcript and in the key alike. The only product named is monday itself, in the Consultant's question at cue 262 and the client's answer at cue 265, both about having no account.
- The Consultant appears only as `Consultant`, with no personal name and no gendered pronoun. The client refers to the absent founder as `Dad`; the key maps that to his full name, spoken once at cue 29.
- Dates are relative and fit September 2026: the client says "It's September", and the walkthrough is Thursday the eighth at seven-thirty, which is Thursday 8 October 2026. "By Friday" and "tomorrow" are relative to the unstated call date. No transcript states its own date.
- Person and organisation names were checked against every other fixture folder and collide with none; a web search for the company name found no business by it.
