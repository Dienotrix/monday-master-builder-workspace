# s21-existing-account (calibration fixture, C64)

A renewal-shaped expansion call with an existing client of ours, first-hand, one transcript. In an earlier rollout, whose date is in the record and is never spoken on the call, the Consultant built for a market research fieldwork agency: a Studies board with a Friday digest that the account leads still live on, an Incentives board that finance now treats as its own, and a recruiter rota board that the recruiters abandoned inside three months for a spreadsheet. Since then the client's fieldwork manager has built her own study-costing board with a form and subitem cells, hit a wall she names precisely, and walks it on a shared screen mid-call. The only clock in the call is ours: the Consultant says the renewal quote's pricing is held until 20 November, and the client answers "Noted." and moves on. Everything in it is invented.

## Conventions and deviations worth knowing

- The key uses two of the calibration statuses, `met-with-residual` and `not-obtainable`, and the per-criterion `asked` flag. It does not exercise `advisory`, because the call type resolves cleanly; no calibration fixture carries a positive `advisory` row, because every fixture's type resolves; the value's positive case is the constructed unresolvable fragment of BUILD-PLAN.md section 4.3, item 8 (H50).
- The `call_type` value is one of the labels of ARCHITECTURE.md 7.1 and the type-header values are the tokens `shared/record-map.md` pins (the 8.3 step 5 values in their hyphenated form). `intake.type_header` has the shape the other calibration keys use (s12, s16, s19, s20): `label`, `seat`, `relationship`, `direction_of_show`, `output_class`, `room_shape`, `money_live` (each modifier with a `_note` carrying the reasoning), `segments` (three, with the screen share as the middle one), the five `signals` in priority order and `proposal`. The C64 extras sit beside them: `sub_trigger`, `labels_not_applied` (the labels the key says must not be applied), `relationship_evidence` and `agenda`. `direction_of_show` carries one of the four tokens, `no-show`, the dominant one; a `direction_of_show_note` beside it points at the middle segment, which carries `they-show-candidate` with its boundaries, so a grader that compares exact strings reads the vocabulary and never a composite.
- Objection types lead with a section 5.2 name or `Outside the map:`, as in the reviewed keys. C81's "Changing what you built for us costs us" is treated as a named row.
- To stay under the 400-line budget, every list entry and every second-level key is printed on one line. The field set and order are unchanged from s1 to s10.
- Tool references are category words throughout, at most one category per sentence, in the transcript and in the key alike.
- The client is UK-flavoured (rota, motorway, telly). No currency is spoken; the key does not add one.
- Dates are relative and fit late October 2026: "the twentieth of November" is Friday 20 November 2026; "next week", "the week after next" and "three weeks ago" are relative to the unstated call date. The transcript does not state its own date, so the key's `2026-11-20` presumes the call date supplied at intake; the transcript alone fixes only the day and the month.
- Person and organisation names were checked against every other fixture folder and collide with none; the consultant appears only as `Consultant`.
- `fixtures-work/_work-s21/` holds the authoring scratch (`turns.py`, `build_transcript.py`, `build_key.py`). They are behind the fixture files now, because the review pass below edited the files directly; do not re-run them, they write straight to the fixture files.
