# s25-client-documents (the document fixture, A16)

Not a transcript fixture. Three client documents and one short call from a fictional independent seed-testing laboratory: the client's own procedure as a Word file, the flowchart that goes with it as a PNG image, a "what we need" write-up as a PDF, and a fifteen-minute call with the person who runs reception, in which she says the documented priority-approval step is not used. The key asserts how documents are taken in beside a call: measured, saved unchanged, author, date and status recorded or left `unknown`, the SOP feeding current state and the write-up feeding the requirement trace, the flowchart becoming `documented` inventory rows with page citations, every extract `[Q/doc]` by section or page, the document-versus-call disagreement kept as a conflict and offered as a callback, and the record graded twice, document-only and then with the call, ending in the same state whichever kind of input arrives first. Everything in it is invented.

Give the plugin all four files as one client's inputs. H56 runs it twice: the three documents first and then the call, and the call first and then the documents, with no new file, and expects the same end state from both. Locations in the key: a call quote is `HH:MM:SS Speaker Name (role)` from the line it is on, the Consultant with no role; a document extract is `QP-07 section N.N` (or `QP-07 front page`, `QP-07 revision history`) for the SOP, `write-up p.N section N` with the W id where one applies for the PDF, and `figure 1 p.1` for the image, never a speaker. A document extract sits inside one paragraph of the SOP, inside one section of one page of the write-up (lines joined by a space, as a text extractor yields them), or is one label of the figure.

## Conventions and deviations worth knowing

- The call date is not in the file. Internal evidence puts it in the last week of September 2026 (the co-operative contract "from October", "next week's the first lorry", the incident "September"); the next meeting is Friday 9 October 2026 at two o'clock. The SOP is dated 11 March 2026 by itself; the figure and the write-up carry no date and are sequenced after the SOP as a tie, from their references to version 3.2.
- Speech is British and no time zone is stated; the two o'clock is the client's local afternoon.
- The key uses four of the calibration statuses: `met-with-residual`, `not-obtainable` and the per-criterion `asked` flag in the end state, and the assertion that `advisory` is absent in both passes, which is the document-specific point (a missing type header demotes nothing).
- `portfolio_expected` is `false` with "no account, no tier" as the reason, as in s2; s1 stays the only key with `null`.
- Tool references are category words throughout, one category per sentence: a spreadsheet, a database, a word-processing template, a paper form, a courier, a shared drive. The monday platform is named as itself; no account exists.
- The image has no text layer, so `check.py` verifies figure labels against the drawing list the image was rendered from, and the key's `transcript_format` says the labels are read from the image. PDF extracts are verified by a byte scan of the `Tj` strings per page, with lines within a section joined by a space; every extract the key cites also sits on one line.
- To stay under the 400-line budget, every list entry and most nested dicts are printed on one line; `intake.grading_passes` is expanded one level so the two passes and the arrival orders read as their own blocks.
- Call types and the type header use the tokens `shared/record-map.md` pins for the type header (the ARCHITECTURE.md 8.3 step 5 values in their hyphenated form, with the fixture's reasoning in a `_note` beside each field) and the labels of 7.1 verbatim; the R names, gate names and per-pain check names are copied from s1's key.
