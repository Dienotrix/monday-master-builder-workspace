## How to read the table

- Status is the outcome of the stage 2 unit acceptance unless the clause is accepted later. A row marked `met (test)` can still end with `Still owed:`, the later moments its contract Accept line names (a real Windows run, a headless row, stage 3). Those are listed in the summary and do not change the status.
- `met (test)` means a named test asserts the clause's point against the contract's Test line as the decisions amended it. I opened every test; where a test only ran a command, or covered a materially smaller set than the Test line names, the row is `not met` and says which part, and whether the behaviour exists (I probed several in throwaway copies of the fixture outside the repository).
- `stage 3` rows already pass against the stand-in templates in `tests/unit/fixtures/plugin/templates`; WP7's real `templates/record` and `templates/other` are empty today, so they cannot be accepted yet.
- `changed by decision` rows name the entry and say whether the changed form is met. All of them are met in the changed form; none is hidden under `met (test)`.
- Test names are exact; `Cnn` in a name is the clause it was written for, and the row says what it asserts.
