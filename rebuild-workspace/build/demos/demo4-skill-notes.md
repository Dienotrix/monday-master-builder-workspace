# Demo 4: notes on the solution-blueprint skill

The solution-blueprint skill followed as written to plan v0.2 of the industrial supplies demonstration's sales pipeline (the AI layer, round two). Each note says where the skill was unclear, wrong or silent, and what was chosen. No ids and no personal names: "the user" and "the two test users".

1. **W1 and F1 in a round-two table (unclear, two rules meet).** "Where it will be built" says to plan the test workspace as `W1` and the folder as `F1` and to say "W1 is referenced" in section 16; the round-two paragraph says the table lists only what the version adds or changes and that built rows are not repeated. Chosen: no `W1` or `F1` row in v0.2's table (both are built, verified rows of v0.1); section 16 says the workspace and folder are used as they stand, and names the built rows this version builds on.

2. **UI step numbers in a round two (silent).** The ledger already holds `U1` to `U5` for v0.1, two of them minted at build time (the departed alert's fix and the switch-off). The skill does not say whether a next version's UI steps restart at `U1`. Chosen: `U6` onward, so no key repeats in `ui_steps[]`.

3. **Assumption ids across versions (silent).** v0.1's frozen revision uses `AS1` to `AS9`, `AT1`, `AT2`, `FK1`, `FK2`. The skill says "`AS1`, `AS2`, … in the order of section 1" and nothing about a next version. Chosen: restart at `AS1` in v0.2, because each frozen revision is read on its own (`assumption_refs` resolve inside the revision) and section 1 restarts its numbered list; section 1 says in one line that the first version's assumptions still stand.

4. **One AI opportunity, two categories (unclear).** The readiness check lists "a sort of each new enquiry by product line and region" as one `ai_column` opportunity, but a category fills one status or dropdown column (G-140, G-144), so product line and region need two AI columns. The skill also gives no way to put AI on an existing column (`B<n>.AI<m>` is always a new column), and Leads already has a Region column set by hand. Chosen: two AI columns on Leads, Product line and Suggested region, both from one new Enquiry text column; Region stays the team's own choice beside the suggestion.

5. **The skill's own `{pulse.B3.C7}` cannot pass the blueprint lint (wrong).** The AI-column paragraph (and G-143, and the record map) say a custom prompt names each column as `{pulse.<element key>}`, and the prompt block is client-facing text the blueprint lint reads. The shared leak terms hold `\bC\d{1,2}\b` in class pain-ids, and the `.` in `B3.C7` is a word boundary, so every `{pulse.B<n>.C<m>}` is a block that no allowlist clears; the payload lint refused staged column keys `B1.C4` to `B1.C7` the same way. Chosen: none of this version's AI columns needs a custom prompt (two categories and a summary, whose blocks hold instructions only), and the workflow's AI step names its columns in square brackets (`[Quote number]`), the form the workflow expert itself uses for templated text (G-131), with section 16 saying the build gives the expert each column's id. A custom-prompt AI column on a `B<n>.C<m>` column has no lint-clean form today: the lint, or the placeholder form, needs to change.

6. **Where a workflow's AI prompt goes (silent).** The skill gives `### Prompt` blocks to AI columns and agents, and the ledger requires them only for `ai_column`, `agent` and `vibe_app`; but the card must show "every prompt in full", and a workflow's "Custom prompt" step has one. Chosen: a `### Prompt WF1` block in section 16 (the lint then reads it as client-facing text) and the notification's wording in section 13, so both texts that reach monday are linted and frozen.

7. **The sample-name check needs monday column ids, and multi-line text trips it (silent).** "Put the sample company and person names through `leak_lint.py payload --kind item_batch` from a staged file" does not say what the staged file holds. Staging column values under element keys was refused (pain-ids again); staging them under the live column ids, as v0.1's build inputs do, passed. A long-text value written over two lines was refused too: in the JSON-escaped column values, a line break before a date reads as `n15`, a pain id. Chosen: column ids from the live map, a made-up key for the two columns not yet built, and each visit log written as one line of dated sentences ("Visit on 20 October: …").

8. **The wall blocked three planned names.** One word in each of two lead names and one account name matched other clients' names under the home (the words are left out here, since they point at other records). Renamed before the blueprint was written, as rule 12 asks, so no approved row has to be renamed at build time. The skill could say that ordinary words (a landscape word, a trade word) are the usual hits, so a planner picks less common invented names from the start.

9. **Real customers named on the call are not walled (silent, a gap).** Rule 12 says the sample rows never use the client's real customers, but the lint blocks only what `exclusions.md` lists, and the exclusion step compares only what the client asked to keep private. Here `exclusions.md` is empty, and one of v0.1's sample leads echoes a customer named on the call; nothing caught it. Chosen for v0.2: every new sample name was checked by eye against the companies the call summary names. Suggest the readiness step (or this skill) seeds `exclusions.md` with the customer and competitor names a call gives.

10. **A "quiet quote" trigger the workflow can express (unclear).** The readiness idea is a follow-up chase "for a quiet quote", and v0.1 defines quiet as seven days after Last activity. The skill's workflow triggers are "an item created, a status changes, a date arrives with its time and zone, or a schedule", with no day offset, and the probes built no condition step. Chosen: the trigger is the date in Next follow-up arriving, at 08:00 in the account's time zone. That needs no offset and does not double v0.1's quiet-quote alert on the same morning. The "only when Stage is Quoted" condition is planned as unproved, with a fallback: build without it, so the workflow drafts for every open quote whose follow-up date arrives. Marked "not from the call" on the card.

11. **"Asks the owner to confirm" when the owner is the user (silent).** The readiness idea asks the owner to confirm the draft. A wait step cannot be built through the API (G-132), so, as the skill says, the workflow sets a Waiting status and the owner changes it. But every v0.1 opportunity is owned by the user, and a round two cannot reassign existing rows (rule 10's spread of test users applies only to new rows). So owner and recipient are one person in this demonstration. The card says what was cut (owner and manager notices).

12. **"Drafted from the dashboard" (the readiness wording is not buildable).** An agent's knowledge is boards and docs (G-116), and dashboards cannot be read back (G-11). Chosen: the agent reads the Opportunities board the dashboard charts, and the card says so.

13. **Where the agent writes, and docs in the key list (silent in places).** The skill offers "an update on a named row, a doc". No doc existed, and no row was a natural home: a note row on Opportunities would count in every pipeline chart. Chosen: a new doc, Forecast notes, in the folder, with the agent granted read-and-write knowledge on it. Three gaps:
    - The key forms in "Writing the blueprint" list no doc key; the record map's `D1` was used.
    - Card item 7 is silent on docs; the card says the doc stays in the folder.
    - An agent writing into a doc is unproved; it went into section 16's build checks, with a by-eye fallback.

14. **The earlier version's departures and fallbacks matter to the next one (silent).** The round-two paragraph says to read the earlier frozen blueprint, the live map and `build.json`. But what changed at build time (v0.1's Close probability fallback, the departed alert, U4 and U5) is in `versions/v0.1/version.md` under `## Build` and in `build.json`'s `departures` and `ui_steps`. The fallback decides the agent's forecast method (Deal value, not Forecast value). Chosen: read `version.md`'s Build section too. Suggest the round-two paragraph names it.

15. **A live automation fires on the new sample rows (silent).** v0.1's New lead alert fires on each lead created, so the eight new sample enquiries send the user eight notifications. Chosen: said in section 6, section 16 and on the card (items 5 and 6), as expected rather than a finding.

16. **Live rows the approval touches (silent).** `ledger.py approval --touches` takes the live-map rows a revision touches, and a round two builds on its own strand's live rows. The skill does not say to list them. Chosen: section 16 names them (the folder and the three boards, all change-with-own-yes), so account-build has them.

17. **Assumption ids from the earlier version leak into the counts (a trap).** `ledger.py approval` counts every distinct `AS`, `AT` and `FK` id anywhere in the frozen file. A first draft of section 16 mentioned "v0.1's FK1 and FK2", which would have recorded two forks on their leaning for a revision that builds none. Reworded to "v0.1's two forks" before freezing. Suggest the skill says: in a next version, name the earlier version's assumptions in words, not by id.

18. **Plan tier when the record has one (silent).** Section 14's rule covers only a record with no tier. Here `CLIENT.md` holds the tier read at v0.1's preflight. Chosen: say it was read at the first build and is read again when the account is checked, and list what the AI layer adds (AI columns, a workflow with an AI step, an agent, a doc), with no credit figure, price or package.

19. **Backfill on new columns (unclear).** "Plan it off, and say so, where existing rows must keep their own values." A new column has no existing values, and the existing sample rows have no source text, so they stay empty either way (G-142). Chosen: backfill on for all three AI columns, and the card says the existing rows stay empty.

20. **Scope against rule 11 (silent).** A sort of enquiries by product line and region begs a chart. Rule 11 says to plan one chart per question, but the user put nothing beyond the four AI pieces in scope. Chosen: no new widget; the chart is a deferred item in section 1.

21. **The form question that feeds the AI (silent).** For an enquiry entered through v0.1's New lead form to be sorted, the form needs an Enquiry question. Changing a built form is a change row with risks of its own (G-48 to G-52). Chosen: a UI step (U6), since the AI piece works without it on typed or pasted rows.

22. **Round two before a first showing, and the stage moving back (unclear).** Step 1 sets `round` to `two` "for the AI layer after a first showing", but v0.1 is built and not yet shown. The user chose the AI layer as the next version now (A27 lets the user set the cadence). Chosen: `round: two`. Step 6 then sets `stage` from `building` back to `blueprint`; that matches the next version, but the ladder reads as going backwards. The skill could say the stage follows the open version.

23. **Card item 7 against v0.1's own UI step (a small conflict).** The skill says the build switches automations off when the demonstration is over, with a UI step only for one that departed. But v0.1's build already left U5, "switch off both alerts", as a UI step. Chosen: the v0.2 card points at v0.1's U5 rather than promising the build will do it.

24. **Two ordinary phrases the lint refuses in sections 1 to 15.** "the approval card" is method vocabulary (blocked), so section 13 says "the instructions written for it" instead. "two hundred and fifty" warned as matching a number spoken on the call, so the agent's length limit became "no longer than one page". Neither phrase is mentioned in the skill; a list of "words the lint refuses in client-facing sections" would save a round.

25. **AI on a native board is less proved than the skill reads (unclear).** The AI-column paragraph reads as settled, but the probes set AI on a scratch board; the one attempt on a native Accounts board was refused for its source type. Every AI column here sits on an installed native. Chosen: an unproved build check with a fallback (the column stays plain, the AI piece departs). The skill could say the native case is unprobed until a demonstration proves it.

26. **The AI notice (silent).** `CLIENT.md` carries `ai_notice: not-yet`, and this version plans AI elements, but the skill does not say whether planning, approving or building AI triggers the notice, or who sets it. Chosen: left as it is and listed as an open item in section 16, not asked on the card.
