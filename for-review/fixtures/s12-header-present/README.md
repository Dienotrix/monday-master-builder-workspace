# s12-header-present (calibration fixture, C57a)

A first-hand discovery call with an environmental testing laboratory, exported with a large participant header and a matching meeting-chat file. Every defect the fixture plants is in the header and in the gap between the two files, not in the speech: the transcript body is clean by every measurement the intake takes (units, collisions, label shape, short and lowercase-initial units), which is the point; the speech itself carries ordinary fillers, restarts and self-corrections, as speech does. The key asserts that the header is treated as claimed and not observed, and it asserts the repair of each defect. Fictional client: a three-lab environmental testing business owned by a group, with a county water authority as its biggest customer. Everything in it is invented.

## Conventions and deviations worth knowing

- This is the first fixture whose transcript states its own date: the export header carries the recording date (Thursday 8 October 2026, Central). The key keeps it as metadata because the weekday-date pairs in speech (Wednesday the twenty-first, Thursday the twenty-second, the twentieth, January first) fit it, and says in two places that a right date is not evidence that the roster is right. The walkthrough is Wednesday 21 October 2026 at 1:00 pm Central; the sub-desk owner is on leave 'until the twentieth' (Tuesday 20 October), so her questions are dated after it.
- Timestamps are wall-clock (`14:02:15` to `14:45:35`), not elapsed, because that is what this export family writes; locations use them as written.
- The key uses three of the calibration statuses, `met-with-residual`, `not-obtainable` and the per-criterion `asked` flag. It does not exercise `advisory`, because the call type resolves cleanly; no calibration fixture carries a positive `advisory` row, because every fixture's type resolves; the value's positive case is the constructed unresolvable fragment of BUILD-PLAN.md section 4.3, item 8 (H50).
- Every list entry and every three-key dict (the detectors, the bleed and the upside) is printed on one line, which kept the key under 400 lines before the fix pass; the `readiness` block follows the same rule except that its leanings, element rows, incumbents and verdict conditions are one entry per line, so the key is 550 lines now. The field set and order are unchanged from s1 to s10, with `intake` and then `readiness` after them.
- Call types and the type header use the tokens `shared/record-map.md` pins for the type header (the ARCHITECTURE.md 8.3 step 5 values in their hyphenated form, with the fixture's reasoning in a `_note` beside each field) and the labels of 7.1 verbatim.
- Tool references are category words throughout, one category per sentence: the lab system, a spreadsheet, the courier, a paper form, text messages, email, the county's portal. The monday account is named as itself.
- The two identifier values in the header are absent from the key on purpose; a checker that expects the blocklist to spell out every blocked string should treat those two entries as patterns and read the values from the header.
