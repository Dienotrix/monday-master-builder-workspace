# s20-large-group (calibration fixture, C63)

A single discovery call with thirteen client attendees, nine of whom speak and four of whom never do. The fixture plants four things a large room produces and a small one cannot, and the key asserts the repair of each. It guards reversal R6: a cross-speaker conflict is never resolved by recency. Fictional client: a wayfinding signage company with a main plant and one branch. Everything in it is invented.

## Conventions and deviations worth knowing

- The key uses two calibration statuses, `met-with-residual` and `not-obtainable`, and the per-criterion `asked` flag. It does not exercise `advisory`: the call type resolves cleanly.
- Every list entry is printed on one line and the flat intake sub-blocks (export family, detectors, risk row, share by side, confidence) are single lines; the sub-blocks that carry the plants keep one entry per line. The key was written to a 400-line budget (390 lines); the `readiness` block of the fix pass takes it to 556 lines in the same layout. The field set and order are unchanged from s1 to s10, with `intake` and then `readiness` after the 25 fields.
- The call-type header carries the tokens `shared/record-map.md` pins for the type header (the ARCHITECTURE.md 8.3 step 5 values in their hyphenated form, with the fixture's reasoning in a `_note` beside each field) and the labels of 7.1 verbatim; `call_type` is `discovery`. Its direction field is `direction` (renamed in the fix pass; the earlier name carried the suffix `_of_show`).
- Tool references are category words throughout, at most one category per sentence, in the transcript and in the key alike.
- Dates are relative and fit October 2026: a Tuesday afternoon before the 20th (6 or 13 October), starting at about two o'clock Eastern; the walkthrough Wednesday 21 October at 2:00 pm Eastern; the rollout from the week of 9 February 2027. No transcript states its own date.
- The header is a roster of names with no roles and no sides. Nothing in the file says who arrived or left; that is the point.
- Real places (Saginaw, Flint, Traverse City) are named on purpose, as the reviewed set does. Every person, organisation and customer is fictional, and the rollout customer is deliberately never named. Every person and organisation name was checked against every other fixture folder and collides with none.
- `fixtures-work/_work-s20/` holds the authoring scratch. It is behind the fixture files, because the passes below edited the files directly; do not re-run it, it writes straight to the fixture files.
